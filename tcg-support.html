Getting started

  1. Open the Scan tab and allow camera access.
  2. Lay the card flat in good light and fit it inside the frame. Hold still for a moment while the App reads the card name, set and number.
  3. Check the match, pick the condition, and tap Add to collection. You can also scan from a photo with the Photos button.
  4. Your collection and its estimated value appear on the Home and Collection tabs.

Supported games

Pokémon, One Piece Card Game and Magic: The Gathering. Card and price data come from TCGdex (Pokémon), OPTCG API (One Piece) and Scryfall (Magic).

Manual card search and price lookup are free and do not use your daily OCR scan allowance.

The card isn’t recognised or the match is wrong

  • Use bright, even light and avoid glare on holo and foil cards; tilting the card slightly often helps.
  • Make sure the card name and the collector number (usually at the bottom) are visible and in focus. Remove thick sleeves or top-loaders if they reflect light.
  • Very new sets can take a little while to appear in the card databases.
  • If a different printing or edition is matched, open the result and choose the correct one before adding it.

About prices

Prices are estimated market prices from third-party data and can be delayed or differ from what a card actually sells for, especially for graded, damaged, foreign-language or very rare cards. They are for information only and are not financial advice. Prices and collection values are refreshed from time to time, including in the background when Background App Refresh is on.

Price alerts

Price alerts are a Rip & Track Pro feature. Open a card, tap Price alert and choose your target price. To receive alerts, allow notifications for Rip & Track and keep Background App Refresh on in iOS Settings. Alerts are checked periodically, so they may arrive some time after a price change.

Exporting your collection

Pro members can export the collection as a CSV file from the Profile tab and save it to Files or share it to a spreadsheet app. Your collection is stored only on your device and is removed if you delete the App, so export a copy first if you want to keep it.

Rip & Track Pro, restoring and cancelling

Rip & Track Pro unlocks unlimited scans, price alerts, portfolio value history and CSV export. It is available as a weekly subscription (US$7.99/week), a yearly subscription (US$29.99/year) or a one-time lifetime purchase (US$59.99); your local price is shown in the App.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. If you already bought Pro — on this device or another one signed in to the same Apple Account — open the Profile tab and tap Restore purchases.

To cancel a subscription: iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Rip & Track. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription, and buying Lifetime does not cancel an existing subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
