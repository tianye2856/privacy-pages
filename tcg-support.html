Getting started

  1. Open the Scan tab and allow camera access (no account sign-up is required).
  2. Lay the card flat in even light and fit all four borders inside the frame. Hold still while Rip & Track reads the card name, set code, and collector number on-device — or switches automatically to Multi-Language Hybrid AI for non-English, foil-glare, Yu-Gi-Oh!, or slabbed cards.
  3. Check the matched print, select the condition (NM, LP, MP, or HP), and tap Add to collection. You can also scan from your photo library with the Photos button.
  4. Your collection, total value, and top movers appear on the Home and Collection tabs.

Free tier allowance: 1 free card scan per day, unlimited free manual card search and live market price lookup (never uses your daily scan limit), and 1 free AI Pre-Grading & Centering check.

Multi-Language AI Hybrid Recognition & troubleshooting matches

Rip & Track uses a privacy-first two-layer scanner: fast on-device Apple Vision OCR + visual print matching runs first (~1–3s, photo never leaves your iPhone). When on-device OCR cannot reach a confident match (such as Japanese Kanji/Katakana, localized German/French/Italian Pokémon names, Yu-Gi-Oh! foil titles, heavy holo glare, tilted photos, or graded slabs), the AI Multi-Language Hybrid fallback transmits only the cropped card image over HTTPS through our Apple App Attest–verified gateway to Replicate (google/gemini-3-flash). Images are processed transiently in memory, never stored on our servers, and never used to train models.

  • Use bright, even diffuse light and tilt holo/foil cards slightly away from overhead glare; keep the card name and collector number visible and in focus.
  • AI fallback scans typically take ~8–16 seconds (the first cold App Attest handshake can take ~24 seconds).
  • If multiple parallel variants share the exact same printed card code (for example One Piece OP05-119) or if a different edition is matched, tap Not this card? on the result screen to choose the exact printing before saving.
  • Very new releases can take a short while to appear in the public card databases; unlimited manual search is always free.

AI Pre-Grading & Centering Estimator

Open AI Pre-Grading from a card or scan to take a flat, straight-overhead photo of the card front (plus an optional back photo). Rip & Track inspects Left/Right and Top/Bottom border centering ratios along with visible corners, edges, and surface defects to help you screen cards before paying PSA, BGS, or CGC submission fees.

  • Remove sleeves and top-loaders and place the raw card on a dark matte background for the cleanest border measurement.
  • Every user receives 1 free AI Pre-Grading check; Rip & Track Pro unlocks unlimited checks.
  • Informational estimate only: AI Pre-Grading is a visual pre-submission screening aid, not an official grade from PSA, BGS, or CGC, and does not guarantee any grading result or resale value.

About prices, price alerts & CSV export

Prices are estimated market prices from third-party catalogs and can be delayed or differ from actual realized sales, especially for graded slabs, damaged copies, regional foreign-language prints, or rare cards. They are informational estimates only and not financial advice. Prices refresh periodically, including in the background when iOS Background App Refresh is enabled.

Price alerts (Pro): Open a card, tap Price alert, and set your target price. Allow local notifications for Rip & Track and keep Background App Refresh on in iOS Settings.

CSV export (Pro): Export your entire collection as a standard CSV file from the Profile tab to Apple Files or any spreadsheet app. Because your collection is stored locally on your device and removed if you delete the App, export a CSV backup before uninstalling or switching devices.
