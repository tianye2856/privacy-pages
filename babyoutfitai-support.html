Support

Help for outfit suggestions, AI outfit previews, weather, subscriptions, and privacy in Baby Outfit AI.

Contact us

Email feedback@globalpixelstudio.com for support, privacy questions, subscription issues, or feature requests.

Using Baby Outfit AI

  • Set up a child profile with an age range, gender selection, and a built-in cartoon character.
  • Allow location access for automatic local weather, or set weather details manually.
  • Pick a scene — school, outing, birthday, festival, and more — to get a matching outfit.
  • Tap an outfit to preview it on your chosen cartoon character.
  • Save favorite outfits and revisit them anytime.

AI Outfit Previews

Outfit previews are generated by an AI image service using only the built-in cartoon characters. The feature does not use photos of real children. Generation needs an internet connection and can take a little while; if it fails, the app keeps the basic character view.

Weather

Weather is provided by Apple Weather (WeatherKit) with Open-Meteo as a fallback. If weather looks wrong, check that location access is enabled in iOS Settings, or adjust the weather manually in the app.

Daily Outfit Reminder

You can turn on a daily reminder in the app's settings and choose the reminder time. Reminders are local notifications — you can disable them anytime in the app or in iOS Settings > Notifications.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock, all handled by Apple. To manage or cancel a subscription:

  • Open Settings on your iPhone.
  • Tap your Apple ID name.
  • Tap Subscriptions.
  • Select Baby Outfit AI and manage the plan.

Use "Restore Purchases" in the app after reinstalling or switching devices. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Read the full Privacy Policy & Terms of Use. Please do not enter names, addresses, photographs, or other information that can directly identify a child.

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure your device is connected to the internet when requesting an outfit preview.
  • If subscription products do not load, check your App Store account and try again later.
  • If weather does not appear, re-enable location access or set weather manually.
  • If the app behaves unexpectedly, restart the app and confirm you are using the latest version from the App Store.
