Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: July 14, 2026 · Version 1.0

Baby Outfit AI helps parents and guardians pick weather-appropriate outfits for their children and preview them on built-in cartoon characters. The app does not require an account and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Baby Outfit AI is designed to be operated by a parent or legal guardian. Outfit suggestions are general dressing guidance, not medical advice. Please do not enter names, addresses, photographs, or any other information that can directly identify a child.

1. What the App Does

Baby Outfit AI generates outfit suggestions for children based on the profile you set up (age range and gender selection), the scene you choose (school, outing, birthday, and similar), and local weather. It can preview outfits on one of six built-in cartoon doll characters using an AI image service.

2. Information the App Uses

  • Child profile: a nickname, age range, gender selection, and a chosen built-in cartoon character. This profile is stored locally on your device. The nickname is never sent to any AI or weather service.
  • Location (optional): if you grant location access, the app uses your approximate location to determine your city and fetch current local weather. You can skip location access and set weather details manually.
  • Favorites and preferences: saved outfits, language, and app settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases: subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

3. AI Image Generation

When you request an outfit preview, the app sends the selected built-in cartoon character image, age range, gender selection, scene, weather summary, approximate city name, and outfit description to a third-party AI image processing provider solely to generate the requested image. No photos of real children are used or accepted by this feature, and the child's nickname is never included in these requests.

4. Weather Data

Local weather is provided by Apple Weather (WeatherKit) and, as a fallback, the Open-Meteo weather service. When fetching weather, your approximate coordinates are sent to the weather provider to return current conditions. Weather providers process this request under their own privacy policies. Apple Weather attribution.

5. How We Use Information

Information is used only to:

  • Generate outfit recommendations and requested outfit preview images.
  • Show current local weather inside the app.
  • Deliver optional daily outfit reminders you schedule on your device.
  • Restore purchases and unlock Pro features.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use information for advertising or tracking.

6. Data Storage and Deletion

Profiles, favorites, and app preferences remain on your device unless a feature explicitly sends the minimum information needed to provide that feature (AI image generation and weather, as described above). You can remove locally stored information by deleting it in the app or by uninstalling the app.

7. Notifications

The optional daily outfit reminder uses local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification content leaves your device, and you can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your information. Information is disclosed only when necessary to:

  • Provide the AI image generation and weather features described above.
  • Comply with applicable law or legal process.
  • Protect the safety, rights, or integrity of users, the app, or others.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is intended to be operated by a parent or legal guardian, not by children themselves. The app does not create accounts, does not knowingly collect contact information from children, and instructs users not to enter information that directly identifies a child. If you believe a child has provided identifying information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Subscriptions and Payments

Baby Outfit AI offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly and yearly) and a one-time lifetime unlock. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID Account Settings. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

11. Disclaimer

The app is provided "as is" and "as available." Outfit suggestions and weather information are general guidance only. Always use your own judgment about your child's comfort, health, and safety. We do not guarantee that the app will be uninterrupted or error-free.
