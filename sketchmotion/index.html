Frequently asked questions

Does SketchMotion turn videos into pencil sketches?

Yes. It applies an AI drawing style to a selected 3–15 second video clip. It produces a new video rather than a single still image.

Does processing happen offline?

No. When you choose to generate, the selected clip and editing instructions are sent to an online AI processing service.

Is it free or unlimited?

AI generation uses credits. The app shows the cost before submission. Optional subscriptions and one-time credit packs are offered through the App Store. Generation is not unlimited.

Can AI change faces or fine details?

Yes. Generative processing can change faces, backgrounds and other details. Review the result before sharing.
