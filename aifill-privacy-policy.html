Privacy Policy

AI Fill — AI Photo Editor (App Store name: AI Replace & Remove - Fill App)

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Short version: AI editing runs in the cloud. When you start an AI edit, the photo you selected (which may include faces), the area you marked, your text prompt and any reference image are sent through our servers to Replicate, Inc., a third-party AI service, and prompts that are not in English may be sent to Microsoft for translation. The App asks for your agreement first. Files stored on our servers are deleted automatically after about one day. There is no account or login. The App uses analytics and session-recording SDKs (section 7). We do not sell your data and do not show ads in the App.

1. Who We Are

This policy explains how Global Pixel Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in AI Fill (“the App”). It applies to the iOS app distributed on the Apple App Store (App Store ID 6636523443, bundle ID com.maine.aifill). Section 12 lists what is different in the Android app (package name com.skysoft.aifill).

Contact: feedback@globalpixelstudio.com

2. Summary of the Data Involved

  • Content you choose to edit: the photo you select, the mask or area you mark, your text prompt, any reference image, and for face swap the face photo you provide. Sent to the cloud only when you start an AI edit (section 3).
  • Device verification and usage counters: a device attestation from Apple and counts of how many AI requests the device has made (section 5).
  • Purchase information: the App Store transaction information Apple gives the App, and records of successful paid-feature use for refund support (section 6).
  • Usage and diagnostic data: app events, screen interactions and device information collected by the SDKs listed in section 7.
  • What you send us yourself: emails to support and feature requests you post in the App (section 7).

The App has no account or login, and we do not ask for your name, phone number, postal address or contacts. The App does not request your precise location and does not read your photo library beyond the photos you pick.

3. Your Photos and AI Processing

AI editing is not done entirely on your device. Before the first upload the App shows what will be sent and asks you to agree. If you do not agree, AI editing is not available and nothing is uploaded.

What is sent. Only when you start an AI edit, the App sends:

  • the photo you selected for that edit, which may show faces and other people;
  • the mask or selection that marks the area to change;
  • the text prompt you typed, if any;
  • any reference image you added;
  • for the face swap feature, the photo to edit and the face photo you provide.

Where it goes and why.

  • Our gateway on Google Cloud (Hong Kong region). Requests go to a server operated by us. It checks that the request comes from a genuine copy of the App, applies usage limits and passes the request on. Smaller files are passed straight through; larger files are first placed in temporary storage of ours on Google Cloud so that the AI service can fetch them.
  • Replicate, Inc. (United States), a third-party AI service, receives the photo, mask, prompt and reference or face images and runs the AI models that produce the result. Your text prompt is also checked by a language model run through Replicate to screen out prohibited requests. Replicate hosts models published by other companies and handles data under its own privacy policy and terms.
  • Microsoft (Azure AI Translator) receives the text of a prompt that is not in English so it can be translated into English for the AI models. Only the prompt text is sent, not your photos. See Microsoft’s privacy statement.

The finished result is downloaded by the App directly from Replicate’s content delivery servers to your device. It is saved to your photo library only if you choose to save it.

How we use it. We use this content only to screen the request, produce the edit you asked for and deliver it to you. We do not use your photos, faces or prompts for advertising, we do not sell them, we do not publish them, and we do not use them to train AI models of our own.

Faces. The App may detect on your device whether a photo contains a face, for example to check that a face swap is possible. We do not use faces to identify you or anyone else, and we do not build face databases or profiles. Only submit photos of people who have agreed to it.

Safety screening. Sexual and other prohibited content is not allowed (see the content rules). Images are checked by a classifier that runs on your device, and prompts are checked by the language model described above. A blocked request is refused.

4. How Long Content Is Kept

  • Our temporary storage: files uploaded to our storage for an AI edit are deleted automatically by a one-day lifecycle rule. The deletion is carried out in the background by Google Cloud and can take somewhat longer than 24 hours. Links that let the AI service fetch a file expire after about one hour.
  • Replicate: Replicate keeps the inputs and outputs of a request for the period set by its own data-retention rules. We do not control that period.
  • Microsoft: prompt text sent for translation is handled under Microsoft’s terms for its translation service.
  • On your device: photos, results and settings stored by the App stay on your device until you delete them or delete the App. Copies you saved to your photo library are managed by you.

We do not keep a library of your photos or results on our servers.

5. Device Verification and Usage Limits

The App has no account. To stop automated abuse of the AI service, the App uses Apple App Attest to prove that a request comes from a genuine copy of the App on a real Apple device. The attestation is verified by Google’s Firebase App Check service, and the App then receives a short-lived authorisation tied to that device, which is kept only in memory. Our gateway keeps, for each device, counters of AI requests (for example per day and per minute) so that plan limits and fair-use limits can be applied. These records identify an installation of the App on a device, not you by name. Like any internet service, our servers and our providers also process your IP address and basic request details when the App connects to them. We keep these records only as long as needed to apply limits, keep the service secure and investigate abuse.

6. Purchases and Refund Support

Purchases. Subscriptions and other purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple Account. We never see your name, Apple Account details or payment card. The App receives only the transaction information Apple provides, such as which product was bought, when, whether it is active and its transaction identifier, so that it can unlock the features you paid for.

Refund usage reporting. Apple decides refund requests for App Store purchases and may ask the developer how a purchase was used. To be able to answer, the App reports the following to our refund-support service, which runs on Google Cloud in the United States:

  • a random installation ID created by the App (not the advertising identifier);
  • the App Store’s signed transaction information for your purchase;
  • the number of successful uses of paid features, by feature, with the times of first and last use;
  • the App’s bundle ID and version, the platform, and your reporting and Apple-sharing choices.

These reports never contain your photos, results or prompts. This reporting is on by default, and you can turn it off at any time in the App under Settings › Refund usage reporting. Turning it off does not affect your paid access.

Sharing with Apple needs your separate permission. We share usage information with Apple for a refund request only if you have given explicit permission in Settings (“Authorize sharing with Apple”). Turning reporting on does not grant this permission, requesting a refund does not grant it, and you can withdraw it at any time in the same place without losing paid access. With your permission, when Apple asks about a refund you requested, the service tells Apple whether the purchase was delivered, how much of it was used and our refund preference. Apple makes the decision. We do not use this data for advertising and do not share it with anyone else.

7. Analytics, Session Recording and Other SDKs

The iOS app includes the following third-party software. The photos you upload for AI processing are not sent to these services through the flow in section 3.

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Remote Config. Analytics records app events such as screens viewed, features used and purchase events, with an app-instance identifier, device model, OS and app version, language and region, and an approximate location inferred from your IP address. Remote Config downloads app settings. Firebase privacy information.
  • Facebook SDK (Meta Platforms). Automatically logs app events such as installs, app opens and purchases, together with device information, so that we can measure how people find the App. The App does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it. Meta privacy policy.
  • Smartlook (session recording). Records how the App’s screens are used: screens viewed, taps and gestures, and a visual recording of the App’s interface, with device information. Recording covers only this App, never other apps or your device as a whole. Because it captures what the App displays, a recording can include a photo that is open in the editor. We use recordings to find bugs and usability problems. Smartlook privacy policy.
  • Umeng (U-App). Records app events and device information such as device model, OS version, app version, language, network type and a per-vendor device identifier (IDFV). Data sent to Umeng may be processed on servers in mainland China. Umeng privacy policy.
  • WishKit (feature requests). If you open the feature-request board, WishKit receives the requests and comments you write, your votes and a random user identifier. What you post there can be seen by other users, so do not include personal information. WishKit privacy policy.

We use this data to understand how the App is used, fix problems, measure marketing and improve the App. It is not linked to your name or contact details. We do not sell it, and the App does not display third-party advertising. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may also give us aggregated crash and usage statistics.

Support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it only to answer you.

10. Your Choices and Rights

  • AI processing consent. You can decline the AI processing notice; AI editing then stays unavailable and nothing is uploaded. If you agreed and later change your mind, stop using the AI editing features and email us to ask for deletion of anything still held.
  • Refund usage reporting and Apple sharing. Turn either off in the App’s Settings at any time (section 6).
  • Local data. Deleting the App removes the data it stored on your device.
  • Subscriptions. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Access and deletion requests. Email feedback@globalpixelstudio.com to ask what we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, or to withdraw a consent. Because there is no account, please tell us roughly when you used the App or made a purchase (an Apple receipt email helps) so we can find the records. Uploaded files are already removed automatically as described in section 4. For data held by an analytics provider, we will pass on your request where the provider lets us identify it.

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK or the CCPA in California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on your consent for AI processing of your content and for sharing refund information with Apple, on performance of our agreement with you to deliver the features you request and paid for, and on our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and fraud and improving the App.

11. Children and Security

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete what we can identify.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), the keys for our AI providers are kept on our servers and not in the App, and access to our systems is limited to the people who operate them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
