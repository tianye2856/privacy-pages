2. What the App Does

The App reads the printed text on a trading card from a photo, matches it against public card databases, shows estimated market prices, and lets you keep a collection, follow its estimated value and set price alerts. Card recognition is automatic and can be wrong, so please check that the matched card, edition, language and condition are the ones you hold.

  • All prices are estimates provided for information only. They are not an appraisal, not an offer to buy or sell any card, and not a guarantee of what a card will sell for.
  • Nothing in the App is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Trading cards are not an investment product, and past price movements do not predict future prices. Make your own decisions and do your own research before buying, selling or grading cards.
  • Price alerts depend on the data source, your network connection and iOS background activity, so an alert may arrive late or not at all. Do not rely on alerts for time-critical decisions.
