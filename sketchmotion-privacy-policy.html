Privacy Policy

SketchMotion · Video Pencil Sketch
Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy describes the information used by SketchMotion (App Store ID 6825671200; bundle ID com.zhouying.videosketch), operated by Zhou Ying. Contact: feedback@globalpixelstudio.com.

Videos and AI processing

You choose the video and clip range. Starting AI generation sends that clip, the drawing style and any text instructions to our processing backend and Replicate’s AI model service. Video content can contain people, voices, places and other personal information. Only submit content you have the right to edit. AI may alter people or details in the result. Do not submit sensitive personal content unless you understand and accept this online processing.

The app keeps imported clips, task history and saved outputs in its local app storage for your use. Processing requires network transfer and provider-side storage. Removing a local history item removes the local copy; it does not independently prove immediate deletion of provider backups or records. Service retention and deletion may be governed by the providers’ policies and security requirements. We do not use your videos to train our own AI models.

Result retrieval and temporary files

For completed generation tasks, the processing backend keeps a private cloud copy of the result for a retrieval period of about 48 hours, after which scheduled cleanup removes that result copy. Download links are short-lived and may need to be refreshed in the app. A result already saved on your device is not automatically removed by this cloud retrieval limit. Processing source files on Replicate are submitted for deletion after a task reaches a terminal state, with retries where possible; this does not guarantee immediate deletion of every provider log, backup or processing record.

Account, generation and purchase records

Firebase anonymous authentication creates a technical user identifier for generation tasks, credit balances and purchase reconciliation. Our backend can store this identifier, task status, timestamps, requested style, duration, credit ledger entries and verified App Store transaction details. These records help deliver outputs, prevent duplicate charges, return credits after failed processing and detect abuse. Apple processes payments; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Analytics and diagnostics

We integrate Firebase and Umeng for app usage analytics and diagnostics. Events may include app opens, screen views, onboarding steps, style selections, import and generation outcomes, export outcomes and purchase flow results. Related technical data may include app and operating-system version, device characteristics, language, timestamps, anonymous identifiers and network information such as IP addresses processed by the services. We aim to keep video contents, text prompts, access tokens and raw purchase receipts out of analytics event parameters.

Analytics collection can be controlled in the app’s Settings where available. Core processing, security and transaction records can still be needed to provide purchased services. The app does not condition access on a favorable rating or review.

Permissions

Photos access allows you to choose source videos and save completed results. Files import lets you select a video from a document provider. You can manage system permissions in iOS Settings. Declining optional photo-library access does not grant access to the rest of your library.

Service providers and international processing

Services used include Apple (purchases and system services), Google Firebase (authentication, backend storage, analytics and diagnostics), Umeng (analytics) and Replicate (AI video processing). Processing may take place outside your country. Review their policies: Apple, Firebase, Umeng and Replicate.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local content stays until you delete it or remove the app, subject to your device and backup settings. Server task and credit records are retained as needed to deliver service, reconcile payments, handle refunds, prevent abuse and meet legal requirements. Do not rely on generation history as your only backup; export results you want to keep.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact feedback@globalpixelstudio.com and identify SketchMotion. Include the technical account identifier shown in the app if available. Do not send private videos, passwords, API tokens or full payment credentials. We may need to verify that a request relates to your records. Some transaction or security records may need to be retained. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Security and children

We use platform and transport security and restrict backend operations to authenticated requests. No internet system can guarantee absolute security. SketchMotion is a general-purpose creative app and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child supplied personal data, contact us.

Policy updates

We may update this page when app functionality or services change. The effective date identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected here and, when appropriate, in the app.
