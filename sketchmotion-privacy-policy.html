Privacy Policy

SketchMotion · Video to Sketch
Effective date: October 22, 2026

You choose a short video, and the app sends that clip and your drawing instructions directly to Replicate for online AI processing. Task history and downloaded results stay on your device. Credits sync through your Apple iCloud account. No separate SketchMotion sign-up or generation server is required.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to SketchMotion, operated by Zhou Ying (App Store ID 6825671200; bundle ID com.zhouying.videosketch). Contact feedback@globalpixelstudio.com.

2. Videos and AI processing

The app uploads only the clip you select for a generation, its drawing style and any text instructions you provide. Video may include people, voices and other personal information. Replicate processes this content on remote servers over HTTPS; rendering does not happen entirely on your iPhone. Upload only content you have permission to edit. AI can change faces, motion and details.

We do not keep source videos or generated videos on a generation server of our own, publish them, sell them, or use them to train our own models. Replicate handles processing files under its own privacy policy and data-retention rules. Its documented API default removes prediction inputs, outputs and logs after about one hour. This is not a promise of immediate deletion of every provider processing record or backup. Separate uploaded-file storage can have its own lifecycle.

3. Local history and results

Imported clips, task status and downloaded results are stored in the app's local storage. Deleting a history item removes its local files. Exported copies in Photos or Files are managed separately by you. Provider download links are temporary; keep the app available to retrieve a completed result and export anything you want to retain. We do not provide a permanent cloud video library or a 48-hour result archive. Device backups follow your Apple settings.

4. Purchases, credits and iCloud

Apple processes purchases through StoreKit; we do not receive full payment card details. The app verifies App Store transactions on device and keeps a credit ledger, transaction identifiers, generation charges and failed-task credit returns. Keychain provides durable local storage. Small credit records synchronize through iCloud key-value storage when the service is available under your Apple account. Videos, prompts and provider credentials are not placed in the iCloud credit ledger.

iCloud synchronization can take time and depends on your account and system settings. Changing Apple accounts changes which cloud records are available. No Firebase anonymous account, Firestore credit database or custom credit server is used.

5. Refund usage evidence

The existing RefundGuard service receives verified signed App Store purchase proofs and related usage records: app/install or transaction identifiers, purchase and task timestamps, model or feature name, clip duration, charged and returned credits, processing requests, completion and export status, and app version. It does not need your source video, generated video, prompt or payment-card details.

When you separately agree to share purchase consumption data with Apple, RefundGuard can use the purchase-linked evidence to respond to Apple's refund consumption requests. You can decline or withdraw this consent in Settings without losing purchased generation access. For an actually used purchase, we may express a preference that Apple decline a refund, while reporting actual delivery, failures and returned credits accurately. Apple makes the refund decision. Declining consent does not itself prevent you requesting an Apple refund. Transaction verification and operational records may still be needed to deliver purchases or investigate a support request.

6. Analytics

Firebase Analytics and Umeng collect app interaction events such as app opens, onboarding, style selection, import, generation and export outcomes, and purchase-flow events. Technical data can include installation or device identifiers, app/OS version, device model, language, timestamps and approximate region inferred by the services from network information. The app does not request precise location permission, collect IDFA, show advertising, or track you across other companies' apps for advertising. Videos, prompts, provider credentials and signed purchase proofs are excluded from analytics event fields.

You can disable analytics in the app's Settings. This does not disable core purchase records, AI transfers you request or necessary service-security processing. See Firebase's privacy information and Umeng's policy.

7. Permissions and choices

Photos and Files let you choose a video and export a result. Camera and microphone are used only when you choose to record a source video. System permissions can be managed in iOS Settings. We do not scan or upload your entire photo library. Ratings and reviews are optional and do not unlock purchased features.

8. Retention, security and requests

Local task data stays until you delete it or the app, subject to your device backups. Keychain and iCloud credit records can outlast an installation. Purchase and RefundGuard records are retained as needed for delivery, refunds, fraud prevention and legal requirements; deleting a video does not delete transaction records. Providers may process information outside your country. No internet or device-storage system guarantees absolute security.

For access, correction, deletion or consent questions, email support and identify SketchMotion. Include a task or transaction reference if available, without sending videos, passwords, API tokens or full payment information. We may need to verify ownership of the affected records. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription. See Apple's privacy policy.

9. Children and changes

SketchMotion is a general-purpose creative app and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if a child supplied personal information. We may update this policy when the app or its services change; the effective date identifies the current version.
