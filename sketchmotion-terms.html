AI credits

Daily Artist supports 3–15 second clips at 5 credits per second, rounding duration up to a whole second. The app displays a task’s cost before submission. A failed generation returns the task’s charged credits. A new generation or style request has a new cost; saving an existing completed result does not. Granted credits do not expire under the current product offer. Service access and balances remain subject to valid transactions, refunds, abuse prevention and applicable law.

Memberships

Optional auto-renewable Basic, Plus and Pro subscriptions provide the credit allocation and billing period shown before purchase. Weekly allocations are granted with each verified weekly payment. The annual allocation is the full year’s total, granted once with each annual payment; it is not a monthly allocation. Subscriptions do not provide unlimited generation. Credit packs are one-time consumable purchases.

Apple charges your account when you confirm a purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings. Your App Store purchase confirmation shows the actual currency, price and applicable taxes. No free trial is promised unless explicitly shown in your App Store offer.

Restoration and records

Use Restore Purchases to reconcile eligible App Store subscriptions. Consumable purchases differ from subscriptions and are not restored through StoreKit in the same way. The app uses a technical account and server records to maintain credits. Contact support before replacing an installation or account if you need help preserving a balance. Keep exports of results you want to retain.

Temporary cloud results

Completed results are available for cloud retrieval for about 48 hours before scheduled cleanup. Download links are temporary. Export results you want to keep; the service does not provide permanent cloud backup. A local exported video is not automatically removed by the cloud retrieval limit. Necessary transaction and security records can remain after a video result is deleted.

AI processing needs an internet connection and relies on third-party services. Processing time, output and availability can vary. We cannot guarantee uninterrupted service or that every clip will achieve your desired style. Contact feedback@globalpixelstudio.com for support. Apple payment refund requests go through Apple.
