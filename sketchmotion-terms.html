2. What the app does

Choose or record a source video, select a supported clip range and drawing style, and review its credit estimate before starting. Daily Artist supports 3–15 seconds at 5 credits per second; Detail Artist supports 3–10 seconds at 12 credits per second. Duration rounds up to whole seconds for credit calculation. The app shows the applicable model and cost. This is video-to-video editing; a text instruction does not replace a source clip.
