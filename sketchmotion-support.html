SketchMotion Support

Help with video pencil sketches, credits and purchases.

Contact feedback@globalpixelstudio.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and a description of the issue. For generation issues, include the task identifier if available. Do not email private source videos or access tokens.

How do I turn a video into a pencil sketch?

Choose a video from Photos or Files, trim a 3–15 second clip, select Pencil Sketch, Colored Pencil, Graphite or Line Art, then review the credit cost and start generation. Processing uses online AI and requires a network connection.

How many credits does a clip use?

Daily Artist uses 5 credits per second, with duration rounded up to whole seconds. A 3-second clip uses 15 credits; a 7.5-second clip uses 40; a 15-second clip uses 75. A new style generation is a new task. Viewing or saving an existing completed result does not start a new generation.

What do the memberships include?

Basic, Plus and Pro offer different credit allocations. The purchase screen shows the exact credits, billing period and App Store price. Weekly credits are granted with each verified weekly payment. Annual credits are granted once per annual payment for the whole year, rather than monthly. One-time credit packs do not renew. Memberships do not include unlimited AI generation.

My generation failed or is still running

Check History after reconnecting. Failed generation tasks return the credits charged for that task. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same clip while a task is still processing. Contact support with the task identifier if status or credits remain inconsistent. An export problem can be retried from a completed result.

How long can I retrieve a result?

Completed results download into local app storage. Replicate links are temporary; its documented API default removes prediction content after about one hour. Keep the app available to retrieve a completed task and export videos you want to retain. We do not maintain a 48-hour cloud result archive. Deleting a task does not remove a copy you exported separately.

How do credits sync and purchases restore?

Credits use a local ledger synchronized through your Apple iCloud account when available. Sync can take time. Use Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple account used for purchase to recheck eligible transactions and active subscriptions. The same transaction does not grant credits twice. Consumable balances rely on the existing local/iCloud records, rather than subscription-style restoration. Contact support before buying again if a balance is missing.

How do I cancel or request an Apple refund?

Manage subscriptions in Apple’s subscription settings. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription. For payment refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem service; Apple decides refund eligibility. We cannot directly cancel or refund an Apple payment.

Why does the result look different?

AI video editing is generative. Faces, hands, textures, motion and other details may change. Good lighting, clear subjects and short steady clips can make style instructions easier to interpret. SketchMotion cannot guarantee identity preservation or a particular output.

Privacy

Video processing is online. Read the Privacy Policy before generating with personal content. For privacy or deletion requests, contact support and identify SketchMotion.
